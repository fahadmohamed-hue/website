About

A career spent proving FM is a boardroom discipline

Fahad Mohamed currently serves as Director – Facilities Management at Emirates Properties Group, where he also leads the construction-to-operations transition for AZHA Community and the infrastructure upgrade programme for Emirates City Master Community. It is the latest chapter in a career built on a single conviction: facilities management is not maintenance work, it is a strategic lever for enterprise value, sustainability, and stakeholder trust.

Across roles at Adeeb Group, Deyaar Development, and Dubai South, Fahad has governed portfolios spanning commercial, residential, retail, education, government, aviation, and master-community assets — building the kind of operational and commercial fluency that comes only from sitting across the table from boards, regulators, owners' committees, and clients, simultaneously.

He is a Chartered Member of IWFM, a MEFMA Certified Trainer actively shaping FM professional standards across the UAE, and Dubai Quality Award recipient (2017) for Operational Excellence and Community Satisfaction. His current focus — and the subject of his book — is what ESG, digital transformation, and disciplined governance look like when applied at real scale, not in theory.

POV

ESG Is Not a Compliance Checkbox — It's a Retention Strategy

The FM leaders who treat ESG as paperwork lose contracts to the ones who treat it as a client relationship tool. Fahad's view: energy savings and carbon reporting only matter once they're translated into the language a client's board already speaks — risk, cost, and reputation.

POV

What AI Actually Changes in Facilities Management

Not headcount. Predictive maintenance and digital-twin analytics don't replace technicians — they change what a technician's morning looks like. Fahad argues the real transformation is in decision speed, not automation for its own sake.

POV

Governing AED 4B in Community Assets Taught Him This About Trust

Owners' committees don't trust a service provider because of a glossy report. They trust a pattern of decisions made the same way under pressure as they are in calm months. Governance, in Fahad's experience, is a habit long before it's a framework.

POV

Construction-to-Operations: Where Most Handovers Quietly Fail

The gap between a building's design intent and its first year of operations is where most long-term cost problems are born — and where most FM leaders are brought in too late to fix them. Fahad's mobilisations start before the concrete does.

The ESG Blueprint for Facilities Management

A Practitioner's Guide to Sustainable Operations, Communities & Legacies

"ESG is no longer a boardroom abstraction. It is the daily work of the people who run our buildings."

Buildings account for nearly 40% of global energy use and 38% of energy-related CO₂ emissions — more than every car, ship, and aircraft combined. Yet the professionals who run those buildings remain almost absent from the sustainability conversation.

Written from more than two decades of operational experience across UAE master communities and large-scale infrastructure, this guide closes the gap between what ESG frameworks require and what actually happens at the meter, the valve, and the switch. Every chapter opens in an operations room, not a boardroom.

  • The Environmental Engine — energy, water, waste, and the path to carbon-neutral operations
  • The Social Infrastructure — indoor environment quality, workforce, and community at scale
  • The Governance Engine — data, procurement, and reporting that survives scrutiny
  • UAE context, five field case studies, a full FM ESG audit checklist, and regulatory reference
